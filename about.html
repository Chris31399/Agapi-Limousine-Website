Our Story

About Agapi Limousine

A family-owned car service built on one simple belief: every passenger deserves to be treated like family.

Who We Are

More Than a Car Service

Agapi Limousine was founded right here in Naperville by a family that believes transportation should be personal, professional, and pressure-free. The name "Agapi" (αγάπη) is the Greek word for love — and that love is reflected in how we treat every single customer.

We started small — a single vehicle, a commitment to showing up on time, and a genuine care for the people we drove. Word spread through Naperville, Aurora, and the western suburbs, and our family grew alongside our business.

Today we serve hundreds of clients — from executives catching early flights to families celebrating once-in-a-lifetime events. We're still family-owned, still local, and still personally invested in every ride we provide.
